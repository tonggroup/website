Optimal transport provides powerful mathematical tools for comparing distributions and understanding data geometry. We develop efficient computational methods and applications to single-cell biology and generative modeling.

## Research Focus

- **Computational Frameworks**: Efficient algorithms for Wasserstein flows and transport problems
- **Single-Cell Dynamics**: Using optimal transport to infer cellular trajectories and dynamics
- **Earth Mover's Distance**: Graph signal processing applications to biological networks
