We apply machine learning techniques to understand and design proteins. Our research includes SE(3)-invariant flow matching for backbone generation and all-atom diffusion models for crystal structure prediction.

## Current Projects

- **Protein Backbone Generation**: SE(3)-stochastic flow matching for generating novel protein structures
- **Crystal Structure Prediction**: All-atom diffusion models (OXtal) for organic and inorganic crystals
- **Sequence-Structure Codesign**: Joint optimization of protein sequences and 3D structures
