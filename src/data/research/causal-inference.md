Understanding causal relationships in complex biological systems is crucial for discovering therapeutic targets. We develop scalable methods for causal inference in high-dimensional systems.

## Research Topics

- **Gene Regulatory Networks**: Inferring causal relationships between genes using GFlowNets and other methods
- **Dynamic Causal Models**: Bayesian approaches to discovering time-varying causal structures
- **Non-Gradient Dynamics**: Learning dynamics that don't follow standard gradient flows
