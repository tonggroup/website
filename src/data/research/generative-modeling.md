Our group develops state-of-the-art generative models including diffusion models and flow-based approaches. We focus on both theoretical foundations and practical applications, from image generation to molecular design.

## Key Research Areas

- **Diffusion Models**: Progressive inference-time annealing, score-based models, sampling from complex distributions
- **Flow Matching**: Optimal transport-based generative modeling, stochastic flow matching
- **Applications**: Protein design, crystal structure prediction, molecular generation
