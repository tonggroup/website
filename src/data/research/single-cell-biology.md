We develop machine learning methods to understand how cells develop, respond to stimuli, and transition between states. Our tools help researchers extract mechanistic insights from single-cell data.

## Applications

- **Cellular Trajectory Inference**: Learning continuous cell state transitions using neural ODEs and optimal transport
- **Perturbation Analysis**: Quantifying cell responses to experimental treatments
- **Disease Mechanisms**: Understanding inflammatory processes and disease progression at single-cell resolution
