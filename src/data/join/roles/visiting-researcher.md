We regularly host visiting researchers — from PhD students spending a semester to faculty on sabbatical. Visits are most productive when there is a clear, shared research question. If you have an existing collaboration with the group or a concrete proposal, please reach out with a short description of the planned work, preferred dates, and funding source.
