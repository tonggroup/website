---
question: How do I apply for a PhD position?
category: Application Process
sortOrder: 2
---

PhD students in the group are typically enrolled through a partnered Vienna-area doctoral program or university doctoral school. The exact program depends on your background and the timing of your application. Please reach out by email first — include your CV, a short research statement, transcripts, and the names of 1–2 references. We can then discuss which PhD program fits best and the relevant deadlines, which usually fall between November and January for a start the following autumn.
