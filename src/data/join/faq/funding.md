---
question: Is funding guaranteed for the full PhD?
category: Funding
sortOrder: 5
---

Yes. PhD positions in the group are fully funded for the expected duration of the PhD (typically 3–4 years) through Aithyra, which is funded by the Boehringer Ingelheim Foundation. You do not need to secure your own funding to join as a PhD student. Postdoc positions are similarly fully funded. For visiting researchers, we expect you to bring your own funding or sabbatical support, though we can assist with invitation letters.
