question: Do I need a biology or chemistry background to join?
category: Eligibility
sortOrder: 1

No. Many of our strongest contributors come from machine learning, physics, or applied math backgrounds with no prior biology experience. What matters is curiosity about the application domain and willingness to engage with the scientific problems. We will help you get up to speed on the biological and chemical context — what we need from you is strong ML and mathematical foundations.
