We are co-organizing the [Non-Autoregressive Language Models workshop](https://pengzhangzhi.github.io/NonAR-LM/) at [COLM 2026](https://colmweb.org/) in San Francisco on October 9.

Submissions are due **June 23, 2026 (AoE)** via OpenReview. We welcome work on non-autoregressive approaches to language modeling and look forward to seeing you in San Francisco.
