Six papers from the group were accepted to ICLR 2026 (**2 Oral, 4 Poster**):

- **PAPL: Discrete diffusion training** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23405)) — led by Fred & Zack.
- **OXtal: Crystal structure prediction** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06987)) — led by Emily & Andrei.
- **FALCON: Efficient Boltzmann generators** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09914)).
- **RegFlow: Efficient Boltzmann generators** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01158)) — led by Danyal.
- **BranchSBM: Branched generative modeling** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09007)) — led by Sophia.
- **Topological FM** — led by Kacper.

Congratulations to all authors and collaborators!
