Three papers from the group were accepted to NeurIPS 2025:

- **Curly-FM: Non-gradient dynamics** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26645)) — led by Katarina & Lazar.
- **Prose: Transferable amortized sampling** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18175)) — led by Charlie & Majdi.
- **PITA: Annealing diffusion for Boltzmann sampling** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.16471)) — led by Tara & Yoon. Accepted as a **spotlight**.

Congratulations to all authors and collaborators!
